Execution & fair comparisons

Signals use the previous two completed observations and execute at the next observation's price. This is a daily-price approximation, not an executable order simulation. Mutual fund cut-offs, payment availability, settlement delays, exchange spreads and liquidity are not modelled. Scheduled holidays move to the next observed date. Daily SIP divides each monthly budget across available observations. No borrowing, leverage, short selling, or interest on cash.

Each run compares identical monthly deposits. Partial months receive a full monthly budget. Missing data are not interpolated. Inspect coverage before interpreting results. Max drawdown uses a contribution-adjusted portfolio return series, including cash.

Tax estimates & historical rules

Optional eligible Indian equity estimates use FIFO lots, a holding period over one calendar year for long-term gains, and 4% cess. Sales before 23 July 2024 use 15% short-term / 10% long-term rates; later sales use 20% / 12.5%. Annual long-term allowance is ₹1 lakh, increasing to ₹1.25 lakh for FY 2024–25 onward. Tax is reserved immediately on each positive realised gain; the headline also estimates selling remaining holdings at the final price.

This simplified model does not offset losses, use prior-year losses, account for other investments, surcharge, basic exemption adjustments, rebates, grandfathering, or actual tax-payment dates. It may overstate tax. Purchases before 2019 are blocked in equity-tax mode. Debt, gold, international and hybrid fund classifications require separate tax rules. Verify your asset's eligibility. Data provenance & market expansion

Demo prices are reproducibly generated synthetic data and never represent Nifty or an actual fund. Mutual fund lookup loads selected fund histories from our database, refreshed daily from MFapi.in, a third-party provider; cross-check with AMFI. Direct/regular and growth/IDCW are distinct schemes. Use growth-option NAVs for this engine; distributions are not modelled.

Indices, thematic indices, ETFs and other markets can be tested via your own daily CSV. Use total-return or adjusted data where appropriate; raw price indices exclude dividends, while fund NAVs already reflect expenses. Index levels are not directly tradable. Currency conversion is not supported; this version displays INR. NSE index redistribution requires licensing. A complete licensed instrument catalogue, automatic ingestion, delisted/merged scheme handling and corporate actions remain roadmap work.

Privacy & beta limitations

Simulations and CSV files stay in your browser memory. There are no accounts, saved portfolios, or order placements. Mutual fund searches and scheme requests pass through our server to MFapi.in. Our host receives your IP; MFapi.in receives the server request and query. Selected market-data histories are stored in our database and refreshed daily. Other funds are fetched on demand; browser responses may be cached for up to an hour. No personal portfolios are stored. Downloaded trade CSVs include simulation settings and source labels. Search availability depends on the provider. Do not interpret a best-performing historical rule as evidence of future performance.
